Who’s behind this

CampusKit started as a group project at UOWD. The idea is simple: good basics, fair prices, no scrambling the night before a deadline. If you have a product you wish we stocked, tell us.

Delivery and other questions

Quick answers before you order. If something is missing, tell us what you wish we stocked.

Delivery

Where do you deliver?

Dubai university campuses, to a drop point or reception desk.

How long does it take?

Usually 1–2 working days inside Dubai.

How much is delivery?

Orders over AED 120 ship free. Under that it’s AED 15. The bag shows how much more you need for free delivery.

Orders and returns

Can I return something?

Yes, unused items within 7 days. Message us and we’ll sort it out.

What’s in a kit, and do I save money?

Kits bundle the same products as the shop for less. Each kit lists what’s inside and what it would cost bought separately.

Do I need an account?

No. Add items to your bag and check out with your name, email and delivery area.

About the shop

Is this a real shop?

Not yet. This is a demo store for a university entrepreneurship project, so the checkout works but nothing is charged or shipped.
